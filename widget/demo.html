DocuPilot demo page

This page simulates a docs site. Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right to ask a question. The widget streams answers from /api/chat and renders citations from the indexed docs.

Try these:
  • How do I authenticate?
  • How do I install DocuPilot?
  • What happens if I hit rate limits?
  • What is the capital of France? (off-topic → expect "I don't know")
